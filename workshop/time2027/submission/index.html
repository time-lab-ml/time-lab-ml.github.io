Submission Information

We invite researchers and practitioners to submit original, unpublished research papers addressing contemporary challenges in web technologies, responsible AI, trustworthy AI, and evaluation methodologies.

Submission Requirements: Submissions must adhere to the following guidelines:

  • Format: ACM double-column English template. Long papers: 8 pages plus references and an optional appendix (12 pages maximum); short papers: 4 pages including references and appendix.
  • Review: Submissions will undergo a double-blind peer review process by expert reviewers.
  • Platform: TIME 2027 uses OpenReview for submissions and reviews.
  • Publication: Accepted papers will be included in the WWW 2027 Companion Proceedings, subject to meeting the camera-ready timeline.

Call for Papers

The rapid evolution of Web AI is fundamentally reshaping how we evaluate intelligent systems. As foundation models, multimodal generative AI, retrieval-augmented systems, and autonomous AI agents transition from controlled benchmarks into real-world Web environments, powering search, recommendation, social platforms, and knowledge systems, conventional single-score accuracy metrics are no longer sufficient. At the same time, failures such as hallucination, bias, unsafe generation, privacy leakage, and unreliable tool use carry substantial real-world consequences.

TIME 2027 invites original contributions addressing the multi-faceted evaluation of emerging Web AI technologies. By bringing together complementary perspectives across Web research, machine learning, security, privacy, and human-centred AI, the workshop seeks to establish evaluation methodologies that are rigorous, reproducible, and aligned with real-world deployment.

Themes and Topics

We welcome submissions across the following interconnected themes and topics (including, but not limited to):

ThemeTopics
Emerging Web AI Systems Evaluation of foundation models, Large Language Models (LLMs), multimodal and generative AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), autonomous Web agents, tool-using systems, and AI-driven search, recommendation, and knowledge platforms.
Trustworthiness, Safety & Reliability Factuality and hallucination analysis, robustness under distribution shift, uncertainty estimation, model calibration, AI safety, cybersecurity, adversarial resilience, and privacy leakage assessment.
Human-Centred & Responsible AI Fairness and bias mitigation, interpretability and explainability, human-centred and user-centric evaluation methodologies, and cross-cultural or cross-domain generalisation.
Benchmarks, Rigor & Real-World Utility Benchmark and dataset design, annotation quality, reproducibility protocols, open evaluation tools, computational efficiency and sustainability, and evaluation in real-world deployment settings.

Author Guidelines

Formatting Requirements: Submissions must be in English, in double-column format, and must adhere to the ACM template and format (also available in Overleaf). Word users may use the Word Interim Template, and the recommended setting for LaTeX is:

\documentclass[sigconf, anonymous, review]{acmart}

Submissions must be a single PDF file:

  • Long papers: 8 (eight) pages for the main paper, with additional pages for references and an optional appendix (e.g. details on reproducibility, proofs, pseudo-code), up to a maximum of 12 (twelve) pages in total. The first 8 pages should be self-contained, since reviewers are not required to read past them.
  • Short papers: 4 (four) pages, including references and the optional appendix.

Double-Blind Review Policy: Paper submissions must conform to the double-blind review policy, meaning that authors should omit their names and affiliations from the paper. All papers will be peer-reviewed by experts in the field. Acceptance will be based on relevance to the workshop, scientific novelty, and technical quality.

AI-Generated Content Disclosure: Authors are required to disclose any use of tools such as ChatGPT or other AI-based systems in the creation of the paper, including text generation, analysis, or other content. If AI tools are used, authors must provide proper attribution and use the tools ethically. AI tools should not be listed as authors; only human contributors should be credited as authors.

OpenReview Profile Completion: Anyone who plans to submit a paper as an author or co-author must create or update their OpenReview profile by the paper submission deadline. Complete profiles are required for assignment and conflict detection. Papers with authors who have not completed their profiles may be desk-rejected.

Exceptions and Accommodations: Deadlines and policies will be strictly enforced. There will be no exceptions or accommodations for late submissions, misconfigured author or co-author accounts on OpenReview, or changes to author lists after the paper submission deadline.
