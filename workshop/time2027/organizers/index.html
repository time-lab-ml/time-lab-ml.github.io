Organisation

TIME 2027 brings together organisers from diverse institutions, countries, career stages, and research backgrounds, spanning Griffith University, UNSW, UQ, ECU, Curtin University, Charles Sturt University, HKUST (Guangzhou), University College Dublin, and Teesside University. The team includes Ph.D. students, early-career researchers, and senior academics, with expertise spanning AI, multimodal and generative AI, human-centred AI, cybersecurity, privacy, data science, and digital policy.
